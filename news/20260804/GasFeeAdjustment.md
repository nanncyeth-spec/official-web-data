## [Announcement] HSK Chain Gas Fee Adjustment

HSK Chain has adjusted the base network gas fee to 0.02 HSK.
<br></br>
During periods of high network activity, the effective gas fee may increase dynamically based on concurrent transaction demand. The actual fee applicable to each transaction is subject to the final on-chain parameters at the time of execution.
<br></br>
This adjustment is intended to support network stability, efficient transaction processing, and the long-term sustainability of the HSK Chain ecosystem.

## HSK Chain Gas Fee 調整公告
HSK Chain 已將網路基礎 Gas 費調整為 0.02 HSK。
<br></br>
當網路使用量較高時，實際 Gas 費可能會根據並行交易需求動態上調。每筆交易實際適用的 Gas 費，均以交易執行時的鏈上最終參數為準。
<br></br>
本次調整旨在提升網路穩定性與交易處理效率，並支持 HSK Chain 生態系統的長期可持續發展。
